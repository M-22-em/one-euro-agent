## Accounting boundaries

The €1 starting point is conceptual and is not counted as an actual receipt. Prior costs were not fully audited; realised profit is therefore not established. The zero-receipt figure is a statement about confirmed project receipts at this snapshot, not an audit of every transaction in the owner's business accounts.

The experiment is successful only if real project proceeds, after attributable fees and costs and completion of the agreed work, meet the €10 net objective.
